Why this guide exists

Less memorising. More “oh, that's how it works.”

Datasheets tell you what a register does. Tutorials tell you what to type. The missing piece is often the picture in between: how a bit becomes a voltage, why a clock matters, or what the CPU actually does when an interrupt arrives.

This guide is here to build that picture. Each chapter takes one idea, makes it visible, and connects it to the next.
