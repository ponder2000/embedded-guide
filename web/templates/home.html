Why this guide exists

Understand it. Don't just copy it.

Tutorials give you code. Datasheets give you details. This guide builds the missing mental picture: how your code makes real hardware respond.

Who it's for

Curious coders. Future firmware builders.

Students, software developers moving closer to hardware, and hobbyists ready to look beneath library calls. Basic programming helps; a little C is useful. No board needed to start.

What you'll learn

Finish with a model you can build on.

  • Trace what happens from reset to a pin changing.
  • Explain registers, interrupts, timing, and buses.
  • Approach your first bare-metal project with a datasheet—not just a copied snippet.
